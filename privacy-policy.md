This is a public personal website for Mehrdad Zaker. It does not provide user accounts, Google Sign-In, public forms, or server-backed AI demos.

## Information you provide

The site does not ask you to submit personal information. If you choose to email the address shown on the site, your email and anything you include are handled by the email services used by you and the recipient.

## Browser storage

The theme control stores a light-or-dark preference in your browser's local storage. This preference stays in your browser unless you clear it. The Game of Life interaction runs locally in your browser and does not submit its state to the site.

## Hosting and technical data

The site is hosted through GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may process standard request information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, referring page, and timestamps for delivery, reliability, and security. GitHub's handling of that information is governed by its own privacy terms.

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser may connect to Google when those files are requested, and Google's privacy terms apply to that request.

The site's click-event code works locally and can connect to an analytics provider if one is configured. The current public site does not load a first-party analytics provider.

## External links

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, Google Scholar, Springer, the USPTO, Coursera, and other third-party sites take you to services with their own privacy practices.

## Your choices

You can block or clear local storage in your browser, avoid external links, or contact the email below with a privacy question.

## Children's Privacy

This site is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly solicit personal information from children.
