**Effective date:** August 6, 2026

**Publisher:** Zaker Works

Trailhead is designed to keep focus activity on your device. The current
version does not create user accounts, use advertising, track people across
apps or websites, or collect analytics.

## Screen Time

If you grant Screen Time access, Trailhead uses Apple's Family Controls,
Managed Settings, and Device Activity technologies to shield apps and website
categories that you select during a focus session. Apple provides these
selections to Trailhead as privacy-preserving identifiers. Trailhead does not
receive or store a readable list of the apps or websites you choose.

## Focus sessions and preferences

Your task text, focus progress, achievements, sound choice, and cached trail
catalogs are stored locally on your device. An active focus session is also
shared with Trailhead's own app extensions through an Apple App Group so the
Screen Time shield and Lock Screen progress can work. Trailhead does not send
this information to the developer.

## National Park Service data

When you load a park's trails for the first time, Trailhead requests public
trail data from a National Park Service ArcGIS service. Like other Internet
requests, that service may receive technical network information such as an IP
address. Trailhead does not add your task, Screen Time selections, advertising
identifier, or precise location to the request.

## Audio and Live Activities

Soundscapes play from assets included with the app. The Lock Screen Live
Activity contains the selected trail and park name plus focus timing and
progress. This content is managed on your device by iOS.

## Sharing, retention, and deletion

The developer does not sell or share personal data. Locally stored Trailhead
data remains until you clear it in the app, uninstall the app, or iOS removes
its storage. App Group data can remain while any related Trailhead extension is
installed. Cached trail data can be downloaded again from the public NPS
service.

## Children

Trailhead is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Changes

If Trailhead's data practices change, this policy, the app privacy manifests,
and App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the affected version
is released.
