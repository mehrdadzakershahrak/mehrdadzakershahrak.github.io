Trailhead turns focused work into a simulated national-park hike and keeps the
active journey visible in the app and as a Lock Screen Live Activity.

For help, email [{{ site.contact_email }}](mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}).
Include the Trailhead version, iOS version, iPhone model, and a brief description
of what happened. Do not include passwords or sensitive task text.

## Quick checks

- **No Lock Screen progress:** In Settings, open Trailhead and enable Live
  Activities. Start a focus hike before locking the phone. iOS controls the
  exact Lock Screen refresh cadence.
- **Apps are not shielded:** Open Trailhead's Screen Time selection again,
  approve Family Controls, and select at least one app or category.
- **No sound while locked:** Confirm the iPhone volume is audible and that the
  selected soundscape is playing before locking.
- **Trails do not load:** Check the Internet connection and try again. Previously
  loaded park catalogs remain cached.

Trail data is informational. For real-world closures, hazards, navigation,
permits, and conditions, consult the National Park Service and the specific park.
