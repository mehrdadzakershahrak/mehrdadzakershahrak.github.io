**Effective date:** August 6, 2026

**Publisher:** Zaker Works

Lady is a family-routine assistant for parents. It helps an adult create spoken
Google Home reminders for morning, school, homework, meals, chores, screen-time
transitions, bedtime, and custom family moments.

## Information Lady handles

When you grant Google Home access, Lady can display the authorized home's name
and compatible speakers, including device and room names supplied by Google
Home. Lady stores the routines you create on your iPhone. A saved routine can
include its name, spoken message, schedule, selected destination, enabled state,
and the identifier needed to update or delete its Google Home automation.

Lady does not create a developer-operated account, include advertising, track
you across other companies' apps or websites, or include a developer analytics
service in the current version.

## Google Home

Lady uses Google's OAuth and Home APIs only after you choose to connect. Your
routine message and schedule are sent to Google Home to create or test the
automation you requested. Google processes Google account, home, device,
authorization, network, and diagnostic information under Google's applicable
privacy terms. Lady does not receive your Google password.

You decide which Google Home structure Lady may use through Google's
authorization flow. Local-network access is used when required to discover and
communicate with compatible devices.

## Children

Lady is designed for parents and guardians, not for children to create accounts.
The app does not ask for a child's name, birth date, location, contacts, photos,
voice recordings, or school information. A parent controls the wording of each
spoken reminder and should avoid including sensitive personal information.

## Sharing, retention, and deletion

The developer does not sell your personal information. Routines remain on your
iPhone until you delete them or uninstall Lady. Google Home automations remain
in your Google Home account until they are deleted. To remove both copies,
delete the routine in Lady while connected before uninstalling, or remove the
automation in Google Home afterward. You can revoke Lady's Google access from
your Google account controls.

## Changes

If Lady's data practices change, this policy, the app privacy manifest, and App
Store privacy disclosures will be updated before the affected version is
released.
