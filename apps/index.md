Find help and privacy information for Lady and Trailhead. Each support page
includes troubleshooting steps and the details to include when contacting support.

## Lady

For questions about family routines, connected speakers, and reminders, start
with [Lady Support]({{ '/apps/lady/support/' | relative_url }}).

[Lady Privacy Policy]({{ '/apps/lady/privacy/' | relative_url }}) explains how
the app handles family routines and connected services.

## Trailhead

For questions about focus hikes, Live Activities, Screen Time, soundscapes, or
trail loading, visit [Trailhead Support]({{ '/apps/trailhead/support/' | relative_url }}).

[Trailhead Privacy Policy]({{ '/apps/trailhead/privacy/' | relative_url }}) explains
how the app handles focus sessions, trail data, and device features.

For other inquiries, use [Contact]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}).
