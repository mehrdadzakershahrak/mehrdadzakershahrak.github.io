I’m Mehrdad Zakershahrak—Mehrdad Zaker on this site—a founder, principal-level AI engineer, and applied scientist with more than 15 years across software, machine learning, robotics, and secure systems.

I currently lead architecture and technical strategy as Founder / Founding AI Engineer at Neural Intelligence Labs. Before that, I was a Staff Applied ML Scientist at Grainger, where I worked on production search, ranking, recommendations, loss prevention, 3D vision, and robotic automation.

My work connects private and agentic AI, production ML, reinforcement learning, human–AI interaction, and robotics. The common thread is trustworthy systems: grounding, evaluation, safety, observability, and operational ownership treated as part of the architecture rather than added after a demo.

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Arizona State University. My research has received 350+ citations, and I am a co-inventor on granted U.S. Patent No. 12,640,000.

This site is the canonical home for my biography, professional history, writing, and talks. My current research program, open systems, and maker portfolio live at Neural Intelligence Labs.
