This is the canonical home for my biography, professional history, writing, and talks. My current research program, open systems, and maker portfolio live at Neural Intelligence Labs.

I work where AI stops being a demo and starts becoming a system people can rely on. That usually means the unglamorous parts: retrieval quality, evaluation, latency, data access, security review, interface design, and operational ownership.

My background spans foundation-model systems, S&P 500-scale production ML, robotics, decision systems, and secure infrastructure. The common thread is systems thinking: the model, data, interface, feedback loop, and operating constraint belong in the same conversation.

I hold a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Arizona State University and have worked across LLMs, RAG, agentic workflows, search, ranking, recommendation systems, reinforcement learning, ROS, SLAM, robotic manipulation, and multi-agent planning.

Outside engineering, martial arts, chess, santoor, and amateur radio reinforce the same instincts I value in technical work: precision, patience, and respect for systems that only reveal their depth when you stay with them.
